This is the classic job, done well: keep the servers running. As Iroh Services grows into enterprise deployments, the reliability of our infrastructure becomes a promise we make to customers — and we're looking for the person who helps us keep that promise. You'll work directly with our head of measurement & operations, and receive support from the engineering team to drive a smooth experience marked by a quality of care & craft that we aim to bring to all of our work.

## What you'll do

- Keep production running: monitor, respond, and steadily raise the floor on reliability
- Keep CI fast and green so the team ships with confidence
- Help define and deliver SLAs to enterprise customers — and build the observability and alerting that let us stand behind them
- Own incident response: reduce how often things break, and how long they stay broken
- Automate the repetitive parts of operations so the team spends its time on hard problems
- Work async with a remote-first team operating around the world

## Our stack

Rust · Postgres · ClickHouse · Prometheus · Ansible

## You might be a fit if

- You've kept real production systems healthy and have the on-call scars to show for it
- You treat CI, deploys, and monitoring as products with users, not chores
- You've worked against real SLAs and understand what it takes to actually hit them
- You'd rather write the automation once than answer the same page twice

We have the engineering skills to ship genuinely new technology; what we need is someone who makes sure it's always up when customers reach for it. If that's the kind of ownership you enjoy, come talk to us.

If that's you, reach out and send along something we can read to get to know your work — a system you've kept alive, a postmortem you're proud of, or a repo that shows how you think.
