### Full-time

We're looking for someone to keep our relationships with leads cooking. n0 is building genuinely new technology, and the people who want it are often technical themselves — so the person in this seat has to be able to hold a real conversation about what Iroh is and why it matters, then keep that conversation warm over weeks and months until it's ready to close.

This is a relationship role first: staying in touch, following up, and making sure no promising lead goes cold because we forgot to reach back out.

## What you'll do

- Own the health of our lead relationships — keep them warm, moving, and never neglected
- Be technical enough to explain what Iroh is, who it's for, and what problems it solves, without needing an engineer in the room for every call
- Run disciplined follow-up: track every conversation, know what each lead needs next, and make sure it happens
- Keep our pipeline and CRM honest and up to date, so we always know where each relationship stands
- Feed what you hear from leads back to product and leadership so we get sharper over time

## You might be a fit if

- You're the kind of person who never lets a thread drop — follow-up is second nature
- You can get technical enough to earn the trust of an engineering audience
- You genuinely like people and are energized by keeping relationships alive over the long haul
- You're organized enough to keep dozens of conversations straight without dropping any

We have the technology and the momentum; what we need is someone who keeps the human side of our pipeline warm and moving. If that sounds like you, come hang out.

If that's you, reach out and send along something we can read to get to know your work — a deal you nurtured, a relationship you built, or anything that shows how you operate.
